Synthetic BankOps review policy v1 — not a real bank policy

Independent review
An operational case must be submitted to a supervisor before it is closed. The reviewer must be different from the case creator and the latest submitter. The reviewer records a reason and either closes the internal case or returns it for further work.

Required documentation
A synthetic operational request should provide a reference, applicant, amount and currency. Staff review the source documents rather than treating extracted fields as verified facts.

AML demonstration
The example AML rules raise review prompts for a single EUR transfer of at least 10,000 and for at least five EUR transfers within ten minutes. These are artificial demonstration thresholds, not regulatory requirements. Staff inspect the stored synthetic transactions and record investigation notes.

Human authority
This application does not approve loans, reject customers, freeze accounts, block transfers or report people to authorities. Closing a case records an internal workflow decision only.
